01 / THE LETTER✦

FROM MY HEART, TO YOURS

A word from
the diviner.

Welcome to the space of fulfillment.

Hello, you...

I started this practice of divination 17 years ago because I was yearning for a space where I could be myself, and where I could offer that same freedom to others, too. I yearned for a space where we need not have everything figured out... Where our uncertainties are met with curiosity rather than judgment, and where even the most difficult questions are allowed to exist.

Over the years, I have somehow come to an understanding that people rarely seek guidance because they have no answers at all. We simply need someone to listen, to offer a different perspective, or to help us recognise what has been there all along.

That is what I hope to offer you here.

Through divination and thoughtful conversation, I invite you to explore the many crossroads of your life. Just like how I understood mine, I am here to help you navigate the future possibilities available for you.

I hope that our time together offers you a moment of stillness amid the noise of everyday life. That you leave feeling a little lighter, a little clearer, and perhaps a little more courageous about whatever comes next.

Above all, I hope you feel welcome to arrive exactly as you are.

With warmth,

Vi

04 / TRADITIONAL HERMETIC TAROT

Opening of the Key

An expansive encounter with the many layers of a question.

A complete, five-operation reading in the Golden Dawn tradition. This extended in-person consultation is reserved for those wishing to explore a significant question through an intricate, traditional divinatory method.

ALL FIVE OPERATIONS · APPROX. 3–4 HOURS · BY REQUEST
PRIVATE ONSITE EXPERIENCERp7,500,000
Discover all five operations +

A specialist reading grounded in the five consecutive operations of the Golden Dawn's Opening of the Key. This is a method-led divination experience, distinct from Vi's more conversational offerings. Each operation considers another symbolic dimension of the question; interpretations are reflective, not guarantees about the future.

OPERATION I

The Four Piles

The cards are divided into four elemental piles to establish the nature of the question, followed by traditional counting and pairing methods.

OPERATION II

The Twelve Houses

The reading moves through the twelve astrological houses, considering the parts of life implicated in the matter.

OPERATION III

The Twelve Zodiacs

The cards are interpreted through the twelve signs of the zodiac and their symbolic correspondences.

OPERATION IV

The Thirty-Six Decans

The decanic divisions allow a more detailed examination of the unfolding patterns and nuances in the question.

OPERATION V

The Ten Sephiroth

The final operation engages the ten Sephiroth of the Qabalistic Tree of Life, bringing the reading into a further symbolic framework.

Your private experience includes

  • Advance enquiry and pre-session consultation
  • A complete traditional five-operation reading
  • A quiet, spacious onsite setting and refreshments
  • A personalised summary of principal interpretive themes

Allow approximately 3–4 hours, with extra time reserved if the full method requires it. Appointment length and venue are agreed before confirming a reservation. Available by advance request only.
